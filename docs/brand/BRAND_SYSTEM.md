# Brand System

## Current expression

Noir Well Health is presented as a culturally rooted, clinically sound, whole-person care brand with a premium visual posture.

## Brand guardrails

- keep tone clear, grounded, and confident
- avoid overclaiming clinical capability or operational scale
- preserve the olive, neutral, and luxury-health palette already established in code
- do not dilute the brand into generic med-spa or generic hospital language
